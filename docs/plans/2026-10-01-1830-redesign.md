# Redesign

**Status: executed 2026-10-01** on the `redesign` branch, without a
step-by-step plan: this records the direction and the decisions.

**Goal:** the same features with a design. The brief
("QuickMeet — Video Call App Design Direction", from the author) asks for
something closer to calling a person than joining a meeting: lightweight,
calm, personal, quiet when everything works.

## Direction

- **Colour.** Neutral chrome and two colours from the telephone: green
  calls and marks who is there, red hangs up. The only other colour is on
  people: a disc with their initial, on one of eight hues picked by their
  name, so a person looks the same in the list and in history.

  | | light | dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | background | `#f4f5f2` | `#111312` |
  | surface | `#ffffff` | `#1b1e1c` |
  | text | `#161917` | `#eceee9` |
  | muted | `#666d67` | `#8e958f` |
  | line | `#dde0da` | `#2a2e2b` |
  | call | `#17794a` | `#45c285` |
  | hang up | `#c5413a` | `#e25b52` |

  The app pages follow the system; the room page is always dark.
- **Type.** Onest, one family, variable weight. It was drawn for Latin
  and Cyrillic together, and names are what the interface is made of.
- **Layout.** One left-aligned column of 600 px, no cards: whitespace,
  type and hairlines. Floating surfaces only over video.
- **The brand** is the word with the green dot that means "here"
  everywhere else.

## Decisions

- Rooms stay the data (M4); the list presents them as people. A row is a
  disc, a name and who is there now; the row is the link into the call.
  No contacts, search, favourites, incoming calls or screen sharing: the
  brief describes them, the app does not have them, and this was a
  redesign.
- "Meeting" became "call" in the pages' words ("New call"). The API, the
  database and the code keep "room".
- In a call the page is the stage. The bar shows the other person and a
  clock; the room id is gone from the page. Status text appears only when
  something needs attention. Alone, the stage is empty and its middle
  says so, with the link to copy.
- On a desktop window the video keeps clear of the bar and the controls,
  which therefore stay visible. On a phone, and in full screen anywhere,
  the video is the whole screen and the bar and controls lie over it and
  fade after four quiet seconds.
- Filling the screen or showing the video whole is decided by how much
  filling would crop: at most a fifth. Before, it was decided by
  orientation alone, which cut a quarter off a webcam's picture on a
  sideways phone and far more inside a browser's landscape strip.
- Your own picture is mirrored, and hidden while your camera is off.
- Rename and delete still use the browser's own prompt and confirm.

## Not done

- The self view cannot be moved or hidden with the camera on.
- No avatar on the stage when the other person's camera is off.
- Full screen on an iPhone: Safari has no API for it. See KNOWLEDGE.md.
